# Sponsor Me, Slayers!

A top-down twin-stick arcade shooter, built in Unreal Editor for Fortnite.

You are a contestant on a dystopian televised game show, fighting waves of cybernetic
hostiles inside a single-room stadium arena while a sarcastic host calls the action to an
audience that has never seen anyone finish a run alive.

By Kailee Nekoba.

## Controls

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Move | **W A S D** |
| Aim | **Mouse** |
| Fire | **Left mouse button** |
| Beg for a sponsor when you go down | **Shift** |
| See your rank and this run's cash | **Space** |

**Moving and aiming are completely separate.** You can run one way and shoot another, and
you should. Standing still is how the run ends.

---

## The loop

1. **Shoot hostiles.** They burst into gold coins, cash bundles and retro household
   appliances.
2. **Walk over the loot to collect it.** There is no pickup button; touching it is enough.
3. **Watch the Hype Meter** on the left of the screen. Collecting things and pulling off
   close escapes fills it. Standing around drains it.
4. **Cross a Hype line and the crowd parachutes a supply crate in.** The higher your Hype,
   the better the crate.
5. **Shoot the crate open**, then walk into what falls out. You are holding it instantly.
6. **Clear the room** and the next one starts, harder. It never stops getting harder.

---

## When you go down

You do not die immediately. Everything slows, the screen drains to grey, and you get five
seconds to save yourself. There are two ways out:

- **Press Shift** to beg the sponsors for a rescue. Whether they bite depends on how high
  your Hype was. If they do, you are teleported clear of the swarm and a med kit lands at
  your feet.
- **Run for the med kit.** One always drops within walking distance, whether or not you
  pressed Shift and whether or not it worked.

Miss both and the run is over. You only get one save per life.

---

## Your rank

Press **Space** at any time to see the rank this run has earned you and the cash you have
collected in it.

Between runs your career rank climbs through five titles as your best score and deepest
room improve:

1. Debt-Ridden Rookie
2. Undercard Filler
3. Fan Favorite
4. Ratings Magnet
5. The Network's Sweetheart

It is purely cosmetic. It never makes the game easier or harder.

---

## A note on the debt

The counter on the television is your debt to the Network. It comes down as you collect
cash and it does not reset between runs.

It is 1,200,000,000 dollars and a point of cash pays off one dollar of it. That is on
purpose. Nobody is meant to clear it.

If you somehow do, the Network will bill you for the privilege and issue you a fresh one.
