## Description (paste into the big text box)

**Sponsor Me, Slayers!** is a top-down twin-stick arcade shooter built in Unreal
Editor for Fortnite. You are a contestant on a dystopian game show, fighting
waves of cybernetic hostiles inside a single-room stadium arena while a sarcastic
host narrates your performance to an audience that has never seen anyone finish a
run alive.

**How it plays.** You move with WASD and aim with the mouse, and the two are
completely separate, so you can run one way while shooting another. That is the
whole game: circling, kiting, and never standing still. Killing a hostile bursts
it into a shower of coins, cash bundles and retro appliances. Walking over them
picks them up, and picking them up fills the Hype Meter on the left of the
screen. Cross a Hype threshold and the audience parachutes a supply crate into
the arena. Touch the crate and you are instantly holding whatever is inside.

**Watch the walkthrough.** The 90-second gameplay video on this page is the build
as of 1 September 2026.

**Why there is no play button yet.** This is a Fortnite island, so it cannot be a
browser build or a Windows download, and publishing one is not just a button. It
means signing the Fortnite Developer Terms, which carries a cost I cannot cover
this week, and it puts the island in front of Epic's own review before anyone
can launch it. I also need sign-off from my studio before I publish anything
under those terms, and I have asked for it. The island itself is finished and
runs end to end. The moment both of those clear, the island code goes on this
page and you can launch it from inside Fortnite. Until then, the recording is the
honest version of what you would be playing.

**Prototype note.** This is a prototype and I am still working on it. One known
bug: the match ends itself when the arena is momentarily empty of hostiles,
because the island is configured as Free For All and Fortnite treats the robots
as rival contestants. Diagnosed on 1 September, fix in progress.

**Built for** the Architecting AI-Native Game Design Documents capstone,
instructor Joshua Berdick. The stat ladders behind every wave of hostiles were
produced by an agent pipeline in the project repository, not typed by hand.
